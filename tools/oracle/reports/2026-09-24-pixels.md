# Pixel diff, 2026-09-24

Branch `agent/trace-pixels`. The draw-call diff misses anything the original keeps from an
earlier frame without repainting it (menu bar, side panel, text). Its framebuffer still holds
those pixels, so this compares the two framebuffers directly.

## The instrument

- `snap` writes both sides' framebuffers, and so does a new `shot` step (no frame run).
  - Original: `TraceShared` v3 `fbAddr`/`palAddr` (`frame.cpp`); `trace.ps1 SaveShot` reads the
    fake ddraw's primary with `ReadProcessMemory` and writes `<name>.orig.shotK.bmp`, 8-bit.
  - Ours: `tests/trace/shot.rs` writes `Machine::present` as 24-bit RGB to `<name>.ours.shotK.bmp`.
- `tools/oracle/pixel-diff.js`: finds differing pixels per region: menu bar, map view, side panel,
  minimap, county panel, end-turn strip (screens.md §0, §4.3), battle overview (§6), and the
  full frame. A channel within 8 counts as the same colour, because each side scales the 6-bit
  palette its own way. For each region it reports the count and a bounding box. It also writes
  a PNG: original | ours | diff (red = differs, blue = masked). Masks come from `--mask x,y,w,h`
  or `tools/oracle/pixel-masks.json` (`{"<scenario>"|"*": [[x,y,w,h,why]]}`). **No masks are
  populated yet.** Herds and flags still count, as a few hundred map-view pixels.
- `trace-diff.js` has a `pixels` category. Its totals are shots compared / shots that differ.
  PNGs go to `<trace dir>/pixels/` (`--png-dir`). PNG and BMP are gitignored (rule 1).

## army-panel-kinds alignment (fixed)

The original ignores the session's **first right click on the map**. This was measured twice:
screen 4 opened 6 steps after the second right click, not after the first. On the first click
at near-0, nothing opened. With a right click on the menu bar added first, the map click was
still ignored. The fix is a harness-only right click at (318, 287) before `near-0`, marked with
the comment `(this harness)`. Our runner now skips such a click, as `script.rs` already
documented. After the fix, screens open and close at the same anchors on both sides.

## Regression check: 2b1da403 reverted (scratch branch, deleted)

Our launch trace had `S011_01` at frame 1, beside `intro.smk`. Before this change the differ
did **not** flag it. The sound sequences matched ([s011_01] on both sides), because each
category was compared on its own. Now a sound is also keyed by how many films came before it

at #0: original f8936 s011_01, ours f1 S011_01`. The fixed build is not. The six scenarios
above show no new sound findings.
