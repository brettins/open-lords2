
### Download

- **Windows:** the `.zip`. Unpack it and run `l2-game.exe`.
- **Linux:** the `-linux.tar.gz`. Unpack it and run `l2-game`.

You need your own copy of Lords of the Realm II (the GOG and Steam editions work).
open-lords2 contains none of the original game's art, sound or data. It finds most
installs by itself and asks for the folder otherwise; `README.txt` in the download says
where saves and settings go and lists the keys.

Something wrong, or do you remember the original differently?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/brettins/open-lords2/issues/new/choose).
