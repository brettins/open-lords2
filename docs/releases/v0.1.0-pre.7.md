The first release since mid-October: new optional battle controls, sharper text,
battle and network fixes, and the first merged change from an outside contributor.

### New

- **Modern battle controls (optional).** Move, attack-move, stop, hold, line, column,
  merge and shift-queued orders, with stances. Turn them on with
  `BattleControls=Modern` in `prefs.ini`. Classic, the original's controls, stays the
  default, and saves and network games are unaffected either way.
- **Sharp text (optional).** Body text drawn at high resolution on large windows. Press
  **F9** to switch it on or off; it starts off.

### Fixed

- After winning a map in a campaign, the next map now starts in play instead of
  repeating the ending. Thanks to @julian-maurel.
- Battle: a soldier who walks into an enemy oil pot or siege engine now fights it hand to
  hand, and the pot keeps pouring on him.
- Battle: an archer knocked down and risen again is laid down by the next enemy arrow.
- Network: games resume when the joiners arrive in either order, a player who drops
  before their first move is waited for at once, and opening a move order no longer
  lets the seats drift apart.
- A tip or letter now pauses the turn timer.
- Lobby: each seat's plaque is in its realm's colour; shields 2 and 3 no longer draw black.
- The front-end Load page uses grey stone buttons, not magenta.
- A marched army's Split button is refused, as in the original.

### Kept from the original on purpose

- In battle, an archer who has just died can still be ordered to fire. That is the
  original's bug and it stays by default (`docs/bugs.md`).

### Known gaps

Closing archers in battle, one AI attack, some narration and some cursor pictures.
Network play is new: please report anything odd.
